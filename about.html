About Rohingya Education School

Rohingya Education School is dedicated to supporting education and creating meaningful learning opportunities for the Rohingya community.

We believe education can help individuals develop knowledge, confidence and skills while strengthening the connection between language, culture and community.

Our work focuses on educational activities, community participation and the preservation of Rohingya language and culture.

Our Vision

We envision a future where Rohingya children and young people have opportunities to learn, develop their skills and contribute positively to their communities.
