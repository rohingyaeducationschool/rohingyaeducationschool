About Our School

Rohingya Education School is committed to providing quality education and creating a supportive and friendly learning environment for students.

Our school supports students in developing knowledge, language skills, reading, writing and other important educational skills.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to support education and promote learning among the Rohingya community. We aim to create opportunities for students to learn, grow and build a better future.

Our Mission

To provide inclusive and quality education, support Rohingya language learning, develop educational skills and encourage students to become knowledgeable and responsible members of their community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a supportive learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, skills, language and confidence for present and future generations.

Our Values

📚 Education and Learning

🌐 Language and Culture

🤝 Community and Cooperation

⭐ Respect and Responsibility
