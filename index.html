Welcome to Rohingya Education School

Rohingya Education School is committed to supporting education and creating meaningful learning opportunities for children, young people and communities.

Our goal is to encourage learning, support the Rohingya language and culture, and help learners develop knowledge and skills for the future.

Through education and community participation, we aim to create a positive environment where learners can grow with confidence.

Our Mission

Building opportunities through education, learning and community participation.

Our mission is to support accessible and meaningful education while promoting the Rohingya language, knowledge, culture and community values. We aim to encourage learners to develop confidence, skills and opportunities for the future.
