Welcome to Rohingya Education School

Rohingya Education School is committed to supporting education and creating opportunities for children, young people and communities.

Our goal is to encourage learning, protect the Rohingya language and culture, and help learners develop knowledge and skills for the future.

Our Mission

Building opportunities through education and community participation.

Our mission is to support accessible and meaningful education while promoting the Rohingya language, knowledge, culture and community values. We aim to encourage learners to develop confidence, skills and opportunities for the future.
